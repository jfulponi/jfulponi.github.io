## About Me

Senior Data Scientist and Economist with 7+ years of experience turning geospatial and mobility data into policy-relevant insights. I have worked with the **World Bank**, the **Inter-American Development Bank**, and private consultancies across **Latin America, Europe, and Africa**, leading projects that range from origin-destination matrix estimation to infrastructure impact evaluation.

My work sits at the intersection of **spatial econometrics, machine learning, and cloud computing**. I design end-to-end pipelines — from raw GPS and mobile-phone traces to actionable indicators — using Python, R, Spark, and cloud platforms (AWS, GCP). I am driven by the challenge of making large-scale geospatial analysis reproducible, scalable, and useful for decision-makers.
